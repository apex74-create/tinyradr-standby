Privacy Policy — Apex Browser Guard and the Apex Clock line

Who we are: Apex Air Solutions. Our home on the web is https://tinyradr.com

This page covers two sets of browser extensions we make:

  • Apex Browser Guard — a safety tool that watches for known browser threats.
  • The Apex Clock line — clocks that run in your browser, including the Apex Clock — Glass House Flagship.

We wrote this in plain words on purpose. Here is the whole story.

Apex Browser Guard

The Guard collects nothing.

  • It does not collect your name, your email, or anything about you.
  • It does not record the websites you visit.
  • It does not send information about you or your browsing anywhere. Not to us. Not to anyone.

All of the Guard's work happens on your own computer, inside your browser.

  • The Guard blocks known bad sites, trackers, and tricks that try to probe your home network. It does this with rules that run locally, on your machine.
  • Your settings — like the sites you choose to block or allow — are stored in your browser's own storage, on your computer. They stay there.
  • Once in a while the Guard may download a fresh list of known bad sites, so its rules stay up to date. That is a one-way download, like picking up a new phone book. It does not carry any information about you back with it.

No selling. No sharing. No tracking. There is nothing to sell or share, because nothing is collected.

The Apex Clock line

The clocks run locally, in your browser.

  • A clock shows you the time and the clock face. That work happens on your computer. The clock does not collect information about you, and it does not track what you do.

One part of the clock reaches out: the weather line.

  • Some clocks in this line show a short weather line. To get the weather for your area, the clock asks our own forecast service at tinyradr.com for the prediction for your area.
  • That is why the Location permission is declared: your approximate area is used to pick the right forecast. Your location is not stored by the clock, is not sold, and is not handed to anyone else.
  • If the forecast cannot be reached, the clock says so plainly on its face instead of making something up.

No selling. No sharing. No tracking. The clocks carry no ads and no trackers.

The short version

  • We do not collect your personal information.
  • We do not sell or transfer your data to anyone.
  • We do not track you.
  • The Guard protects you on your own machine. The clocks tell time on your own machine, and the only thing that ever leaves your browser is the weather line asking for your area's forecast.

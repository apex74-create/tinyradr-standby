2 · THE SHELF — YOUR CANON

These are the facts of your world, written by you. Add a fact, the words that trigger it, and — if you want the gate to guard it — the thing that must never be said about it. Everything stays on this phone. No account, no key, no server.

3 · CHAT MEMORY & SYSTEM NOTES

The shelf holds what's true. These two pads hold the rest — and they're yours to carry into Janitor.
Chat Memory is where the story stands: what already happened, who's where, what's still open. Keep it current, and the story stops forgetting itself.
System Notes are your standing instructions: how a character behaves, scene rules, style — the notes that should ride along with every chat.
Both pads save on this phone as you type. The COPY buttons put a pad on your clipboard in one tap — paste it into Janitor's own chat memory / notes, and it rides with the chat there.

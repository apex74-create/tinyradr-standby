Every face is on the lift.

The faces that hung on this shelf came down into the Garage on Nov 10, 2026 — by the owner's order. They are operational builds: they load, they scan clean, they run. What they're here for is the finish work — photoreal render, one universal Apex icon, dial fits cut to the watch they're under.

Each face is worked on individually, in its own bay, and hung back up on the shelf when it reaches fruition — one at a time, at the same $9.99.

What belongs here: the Garage holds watch faces — builds that carry a clock, a weather call, a camera call, or a Companion call from the screen. Container products and CB-class builds are not watch faces; they keep their own places in the Armory.

Everything on this page is a watch face — every card below says so, in the same shape: what it is, one line, the price. The applications — Android apps, the browser extension and the website apps — live on the home page, in the Applications field.

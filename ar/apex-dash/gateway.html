⚙ Gateway Setup

APEX AIR SOLUTIONS — OPERATOR ACKNOWLEDGMENT & DEVICE REGISTRATION

Step 1 — Operator Signature

Enter your name or callsign to acknowledge this session. This is required before the registry is activated. No typing needed — tap the camera icon to scan a printed label.

Step 2 — Router (Optional)

Look on the back of your router for the SSID sticker, gateway IP, and MAC address. Tap 📷 SCAN next to any field to capture it automatically with your camera.

Step 3 — Handset (Optional)

Go to Settings › About Phone › Status on your Android device to find the IP and MAC. Tap 📷 SCAN to capture from a screenshot or printed page.

Step 4 — Chromebook (Optional)

Open Settings › Network › Wi-Fi on your Chromebook to find the IP and MAC. Tap 📷 SCAN to capture from a screen or label.

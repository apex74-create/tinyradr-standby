You saw something in the sky, and you got it on camera. Good. That footage is the whole ballgame — bring it here and it gets measured, not argued about.

WHAT THIS STATION DOES

Send a photo or a film of what you saw. The station works it the same way every time:

  1. It is hashed and logged. The moment your file arrives, it gets a fingerprint (a hash) and a line in the log. That is the chain of custody — proof of exactly what came in, and that the file worked on is the file you sent.
  2. It is cleaned, stacked, and refined. Noise comes off, frames are stacked to pull out detail one frame alone cannot show, and the image is refined until what is actually there stands up.
  3. The outline gets measured. You get the outline the data actually supports — drawn and measured, not guessed.
  4. You get a plain-words read. What it appears to be. How sure the measurements are. And what would settle it. Plain words, no costume.

Film gets one more pass: the video is scrubbed in both directions, at multiple speeds, forward and reverse, so anything that only shows up in motion gets its chance to show up.

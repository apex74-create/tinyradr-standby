THE EXAMINATION MACHINE DEMONSTRATION

Plain words first: this is a demonstration of the event, running entirely in your browser. Nothing you put here uploads anywhere — no file leaves this page, nothing is sent to the station, nothing is kept. The demo shows you the passes a frame goes through. A real examination read comes from the email station above — a machine here can show you the shape of the work, it cannot grade your footage.

Feed the machine: pick a sample frame, or drop in one of your own images (it is read on this device only, for the demo, and forgotten when you leave the page).

Frame loaded: SAMPLE A — TRIANGLE OVER THE TREELINE (a drawing, built into this page as a demo fixture)
  1. PASS 1 — HASH & LOG. The frame's fingerprint is computed here, in your browser.
  2. PASS 2 — STACK. The frame is re-laid over itself, aligned, the way stills from a film are stacked.
  3. PASS 3 — REFINE. Contrast and sharpness passes — the picture is cleaned, not altered.
  4. PASS 4 — TRACE & MEASURE. The largest single drawn element is outlined and measured — its box, its centre, its size in this frame.
  5. OUT — INDICATED. The frame comes back marked: outline, measurements, and the demo stamp.

Every number below the stamp is a measurement of the demo image itself — pixels counted in the frame you just fed the machine. The machine makes no finding about any real object in any real sky. That is the station's work, by email, on your originals.

THE EXAMINATION MACHINE LIVE SWEEP DEMONSTRATION

Plain words first: the machine now works on your own sweep. Press BEGIN SWEEP and turn slowly in a circle. The face reads this device's own compass and motion sensors, takes its samples on the PE beat — one sample every 369 ms across one 11-second cycle — and converts them here, on this device, into the readouts below: heading, travel, sweep rate, stability, and the sweep's own fingerprint. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and no stored picture is shown: the trace on the scope is drawn from your movement, while you make it. You bought the face; the face carries the machine.

If no sensor answers, the needle sweeps SEARCHING and the readouts say NO MEASURE. This face would rather show you nothing than invent a heading.
